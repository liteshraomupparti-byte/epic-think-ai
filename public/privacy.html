Google API Services Compliance & Data Protection

Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 30, 2026 | Application: Epic Think AI

1. Overview & Commitment

At Epic Think AI ("we", "our", or "the Platform"), we respect your privacy and are committed to safeguarding personal and sensitive data. This Privacy Policy details how our autonomous AI agent platform collects, uses, encrypts, and handles your data when you interact with Epic Think AI and connect your third-party integrations, specifically including Google Workspace, Google Drive, Google Sheets, Gmail, and Google Calendar.

Epic Think AI functions as a user-directed AI agent. Our services access your third-party accounts only upon your explicit authorization and execute actions strictly on your behalf.

2. Google User Data & OAuth Scopes

When you choose to enable Google-powered capabilities inside Epic Think AI, you grant permission through Google's secure OAuth 2.0 protocol. Below are the specific Google OAuth scopes Epic Think AI requests and why each is required:

API Scope Sensitivity Direct Functional Purpose
openid, .../auth/userinfo.email, .../auth/userinfo.profile Non-Sensitive Authenticates your identity and displays your user profile and email in your account settings.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.file Sensitive Allows Epic Think AI to view, create, and modify documents and files that you explicitly upload, create, or open with the agent. We adhere to the least-privilege principle and do not request broad read-all drive access unless explicitly needed.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/spreadsheets Sensitive Enables the agent to read tables, insert data rows, and generate structured sheets upon your direct instruction (e.g. data synthesis, reporting).
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/calendar Sensitive Enables the agent to inspect upcoming schedules, find available time slots, and schedule meetings/events at your request.
https://www.googleapis.com/auth/gmail.modify Restricted Allows the agent to search your emails, read selected messages, and create or send drafts when you command the AI assistant to compose or inspect correspondence.
Mandatory Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance:
Epic Think AI's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs to any other app will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

3. How We Use Google User Data & AI Model Policy

We enforce strict architectural boundaries on how data obtained through Google APIs is processed:

  • No AI Model Training: Data retrieved from Google APIs (such as email bodies, spreadsheet rows, calendar entries, and drive documents) is NEVER used to train, retrain, fine-tune, or improve generalized AI or machine learning models (such as foundational LLMs from OpenAI, Anthropic, or Google).
  • Transient Processing: User content is passed dynamically as ephemeral context to execute the specific tool operation requested by the user and is discarded from memory once the task is complete.
  • No Human Inspection: No employee, contractor, or human agent at Epic Think AI reads or inspects your private Google data, unless:
    1. You have provided express permission for debugging a specific reported issue,
    2. It is strictly necessary for security purposes (e.g. investigating an active exploit), or
    3. It is mandated by applicable law or a legally binding court order.
  • No Sale of Data: We do not sell, rent, license, or monetize your personal or Google user data to data brokers, advertisers, or any third party.

4. Data Storage, Security & Token Vault

Epic Think AI implements industry-standard cryptographic defenses to protect authentication credentials and access tokens:

  • AES-256-GCM Token Encryption: All Google OAuth refresh tokens and credentials stored server-side are encrypted using authenticated AES-256-GCM with hardware-backed encryption keys. Tokens are never logged in plaintext.
  • In-Transit Encryption: All communications between your client browser, our servers, and Google APIs occur strictly over TLS 1.3/HTTPS.
  • Strict Multi-Tenant Isolation: Every record and stored setting is partitioned and isolated strictly by authenticated Firebase UID. Users cannot access or query another user's connections.

5. User Control, Data Deletion & OAuth Revocation

You maintain 100% control over your Google credentials and data at all times:

How to disconnect and delete your Google integration:
  1. Inside Epic Think AI: Open Settings → Plugins → Click "Disconnect" on the Google plugin card. This immediately purges encrypted OAuth tokens from our secure vault.
  2. From Google Account Settings: You can revoke Epic Think AI's access at any moment by visiting Google's Third-party Apps & Services page: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions .
  3. Full Account Erasure: To request complete deletion of your account and associated conversations, navigate to Settings → Clear Data, or contact our support team.
